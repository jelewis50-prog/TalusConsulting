Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy explains what information Talus (“we”, “us”) collects through talusconsulting.net and how we use it. If you have questions, email support@talusconsulting.net.

Information we collect

  • Information you send us. When you email us or use the contact form, we receive your name, company, email address and anything you choose to write.
  • Bookings. If you book a call through our scheduling tool, the scheduling provider collects your name, email and chosen time and shares them with us.
  • Payments. Course and workshop payments are processed by our payment providers (Stripe, and Thinkific for online courses). We do not receive or store your full card number.
  • Online courses. When you enroll in a self-paced course, our course platform (Thinkific) creates an account with your name and email and records your course progress.
  • Technical data. Our website host and domain provider may automatically log basic technical data, such as your IP address and browser type, for security and reliability. We do not use advertising cookies, analytics trackers or third-party fonts.

How we use it

We use your information to reply to you, schedule and deliver our services, process payments, keep records required by law, and protect our website. If you opt in when you sign up or buy, we also send occasional emails about new courses, workshops and offers. You can unsubscribe at any time. We do not sell your personal information, and we do not share it for advertising.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business (for example our email, scheduling, payment and website hosting providers), when required by law, or to protect our rights. These providers may only use it to provide their services to us.

How long we keep it

We keep your information only as long as we need it for the purposes above or as required for tax and legal records, then delete it.

Security

We use reasonable safeguards, including encrypted connections and limited access to your information. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure. If a breach affects your personal information, we will notify you as required by law.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to tell you what personal information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, by emailing support@talusconsulting.net. You can unsubscribe from any marketing email at any time using the link in that email. We will respond within 45 days.

Children

Our services are for businesses and are not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
